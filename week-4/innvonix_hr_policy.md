## 1. General Statement

Management reserves the right to interpret, amend, suspend, or cancel any part of the company's policies, procedures, and benefits, with or without prior notice, subject to applicable requirements.

Management will communicate policy changes to employees. In the event of changes to the holiday schedule, the majority decision will be considered, and any changes must be finalized at least one month before the affected holiday.

All policy changes will take effect from the dates determined by Management. Once a revised policy becomes effective, the previous version will no longer apply.

Only Management has the authority to amend or modify company policies.

## 2. Attendance Policy

1. Employees are expected to begin their attendance from **9:15 AM**. Any time recorded before 9:15 AM will not be included in the average working-hour calculation. The average working time will therefore be calculated starting from 9:15 AM.

2. Regular attendance in accordance with the company's scheduled working hours is a prerequisite for employment.

3. Unjustified absences can delay schedules and client commitments, resulting in additional workloads for other team members. Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance.

4. If an employee cannot report to work as scheduled, they must inform their reporting manager and the HR Department before the scheduled starting time.

5. Employees who fail to inform their reporting manager or HR before 10:00 AM may be marked as late. Leave communicated after 10:00 AM may be treated as a full-day unpaid leave, except in cases where the reason is considered acceptable by the authorized person.

6. Employees are responsible for informing their reporting manager and HR about their absence. An absence of three consecutive working days without notifying the reporting manager may be treated as voluntary termination.

7. Employees are required to achieve an average of **8 hours and 30 minutes of working time per applicable working day**, as calculated under the company's monthly attendance policy.

8. If the required average working hours are not achieved, the shortfall may be converted into an equivalent number of full or half leave days for deduction, in accordance with the company's applicable rules.

### Attendance Shortfall and Leave Deduction

The following deduction schedule is specified in the original policy:

| Shortfall in working time           | Deduction specified                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Up to 15 minutes                    | Half day (0.5 leave)                                             |
| 16–30 minutes                       | 1.5 full days                                                    |
| 31–45 minutes                       | 2.5 full days                                                    |
| 45–60 minutes                       | 3.5 full days                                                    |
| 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes or more | 4 full days, with an additional 1 day for every extra 15 minutes |

**Clarification required:** The original deduction table contains overlapping time ranges and does not clearly explain how the deduction is calculated. HR should confirm the intended calculation before this table is used for payroll processing.

## 3. Reimbursement Policy

1. Employees who remain on the company's premises for more than 12 hours may be eligible for lunch or dinner reimbursement.

2. The maximum reimbursement limit is **₹230 per person**.

3. Employees must obtain approval from their Project Manager for the expense bill and submit the approved bill to the HR Department, along with the reason for staying on the premises.

4. All reimbursement claims are subject to a final review by the Department Head to determine whether the extended stay was necessary for the project.

5. Meal reimbursement is applicable only when the employee stays late for work or when the extended stay has been specifically approved by the authorized person.

6. Employees who arrive early or stay late to compensate for project delays are eligible for reimbursement only if the arrangement has been approved by the relevant authority.

## 4. Leave Policy

### 4.1 Introduction

1. Leave is a benefit provided by the company and is subject to approval. All leave applications will be evaluated based on business requirements and the employee's circumstances.

2. Employees joining between the 1st and 10th of a month will be eligible for leave credit for that month, subject to the applicable leave rules.

3. Employees joining between the 11th and the last day of a month will not receive leave credit for that month.

4. The original policy also specifies that salary processing for employees joining after the 10th may be deferred and combined with the following month's salary. HR should confirm the current payroll process applicable to such cases.

### 4.2 Earned Leave Eligibility

#### Eligibility and Entitlement

1. Earned Leave (EL) is available to eligible staff members. Consultants, probationers, interns, and trainees are excluded, subject to the specific exceptions described in this policy.

2. Eligible employees are entitled to **12 days of Earned Leave per financial year**, which runs from 1 April to 31 March.

3. Earned Leave is credited after successful completion of the probation period and is administered in accordance with the applicable financial-year and pro-rata rules.

4. Employees with more than three years of experience may be allotted four leaves during probation, as specified in the policy. Their subsequent leave entitlement will be determined after completion of probation.

5. Unused Earned Leave will be encashed at the end of the financial year based on the employee's basic salary, subject to the applicable company rules.

6. If an employee resigns before the end of the financial year after using more than their eligible pro-rata leave entitlement, the applicable excess leave amount may be deducted from their salary or final settlement, subject to applicable law.

#### Unplanned Leave

7. The original policy states that an employee may be terminated without notice or payment in lieu of notice if they take more than 13 unplanned leaves, including weekends and official holidays, without proper updates or supporting evidence.

#### Extension of Appraisal Cycle and Probation

8. For confirmed employees, continuous leave exceeding 13 working days may result in an extension of the appraisal cycle and, where applicable, the probation period.

| Continuous working-day leave | Appraisal extension |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 13–18 days                   | 1 month             |
| 18–23 days                   | 2 months            |
| 23–30 days                   | 3 months            |

9. For employees who have not yet been confirmed, continuous leave may extend the appraisal cycle and probation period as follows:

| Continuous working-day leave | Appraisal extension |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 7 working days               | 1 month             |
| 8–13 working days            | 2 months            |
| 14–18 working days           | 3 months            |

The original policy provides examples in which an increment due in January would be considered from February following a one-month extension.

**Clarification required:** The leave-duration ranges overlap at 18 days and 23 days in the first table. HR should confirm the intended thresholds.

#### Administration

1. Employees must obtain prior approval from their reporting manager and keep HR informed before proceeding on Earned Leave.

2. If prior approval is not possible due to an emergency, the employee must inform their senior as soon as reasonably possible, explain the circumstances, and submit the required leave application for written approval immediately upon returning to work.

3. Leave approval is subject to business requirements and the discretion of the authorized leave-approving authority.

4. The original policy specifies a deduction of **1.5 leaves for each applied leave** when a leave application is rejected by the reporting manager or authorized person. HR should clarify how this rule is intended to be applied.

### 4.3 Sick Leave

#### Objective

Sick Leave is intended to support employees who are unable to work due to illness or a medical condition.

#### Eligibility and Entitlement

1. Sick Leave is available to eligible staff members. Consultants, probationers, interns, and trainees are excluded under the original policy.

2. Eligible employees are entitled to **six days of Sick Leave per financial year**, credited on a pro-rata basis.

3. Sick Leave is intended for medical emergencies and illness. Unused Sick Leave expires at the end of the financial year and is not eligible for encashment or carry-forward.

4. Sick Leave exceeding five working days during probation may result in an extension of the probation period.

5. If an employee takes planned leave on a Friday, the original policy states that they will not be eligible for Sick Leave on the following Monday.

#### Administration

1. If approved leave extends beyond the period originally requested, the additional days cannot be adjusted against Sick Leave under the original policy.

2. Sick Leave exceeding three days must be supported by a medical certificate. If the required certificate is not provided, the absence may be treated as Leave Without Pay (LWP).

### 4.4 Marriage Leave

#### Eligibility

1. All employees, including probationers, are eligible for up to **21 days of leave for their own marriage**.

2. Weekends and company-declared public holidays falling within the marriage leave period will be counted as leave days.

3. Extended marriage leave may result in an extension of the appraisal cycle and probation period, as specified in Sections 4.2.8 and 4.2.9.

#### Administration

1. Employees must apply for Marriage Leave at least one month in advance.

2. Marriage Leave must be approved in writing by the Team Leader or Project Manager, with the consent of the HR Department.

3. Marriage Leave must be taken for 21 consecutive days. Splitting the leave into separate periods is not permitted under the original policy.

4. Employees are expected to resume work on the scheduled return date. Additional emergency leave immediately following Marriage Leave will not be accepted under the original policy.

5. Marriage Leave is separate from paid Earned Leave and is not a paid leave entitlement under the original policy.

### 4.5 Birthday and Wedding Anniversary Leave

#### Objective

To allow eligible employees to celebrate their birthday and wedding anniversary.

#### Eligibility and Entitlement

1. This benefit is available to eligible staff members, excluding consultants, probationers, interns, and trainees under the original policy.

2. The original policy specifies an annual entitlement of one day for birthday and wedding anniversary leave, with a provision for half-day leave for each occasion.

3. Each occasion provides for **0.5 day of leave**. Unused entitlement expires at the end of the financial year and cannot be encashed or carried forward.

4. Employees must ensure that their birthday and wedding anniversary dates are correctly recorded in NIMS.

5. If an employee is absent on the relevant occasion without applying for the applicable leave, the absence may be treated as LWP.

6. Employees must complete a minimum of 4 hours and 30 minutes of work to qualify for a half-day arrangement. If the required hours are not completed, a full-day LWP may be applied under the original policy.

#### Administration

1. Birthday and Wedding Anniversary Leave is treated as pre-approved leave.

2. Employees are responsible for ensuring that their personal dates are entered accurately in NIMS so that the system can validate their leave applications.

**Clarification required:** The original policy states both a total entitlement of one day and an entitlement of 0.5 day for each occasion. HR should confirm whether the intended annual total is one day across both occasions.

### 4.7 Leave Without Pay (LWP)

#### Authorized LWP

Employees who have exhausted their available paid leave may apply for Leave Without Pay, subject to prior approval from Management.

#### Unauthorized LWP

Absence without the required approval, or taking leave without obtaining the necessary sanction, will be treated as unauthorized LWP and may result in disciplinary action under the company's applicable procedures.

### 4.8 Leave Approval Authorities

1. All leave applications must be submitted to the appropriate reporting manager or authorized senior, with HR kept informed.

2. Leave approval rests with the reporting manager, HR, or Management, as applicable.

3. Leave requests will be assessed based on:
   - Business and client commitments.
   - Team availability and workload.
   - The employee's leave history.
   - The reason provided for the request.
   - Supporting documentation, where required.

4. All leave approvals remain subject to business requirements and the discretion of the authorized leave-approving authority.

### 4.9 General Guidelines for All Types of Leave

1. Employees must request leave from their reporting manager or authorized senior and keep HR informed. Emergency leave requests must be communicated to the Team Leader and HR by email and phone or SMS before 10:00 AM.

2. Informing a colleague or friend does not constitute official notification of absence. Failure to notify the relevant authorities before 10:00 AM may result in a full-day LWP, except where an acceptable reason is approved.

3. During probation, an employee taking an uninformed leave may be subject to a deduction equivalent to two days' salary for each day of absence, as stated in the original policy.

4. If the approved leave application has not been submitted to HR before the employee proceeds on leave, the absence may be treated as absence without approved leave and without pay until the duly approved application is submitted.

5. Leave entitlement will be calculated on a pro-rata basis, with leave credited after completion of the applicable month.

6. The financial year runs from **1 April to 31 March**.

7. Employees absent for two or more days without informing the relevant authorities, or taking unauthorized leave, must provide a written explanation and may be subject to disciplinary action.

8. During probation, employees with more than three years of experience will be allotted half of the total annual Earned Leave entitlement for the year, as specified in the original policy. Marriage Leave remains subject to its separate eligibility rules.

9. Employees on leave must remain reachable by phone for genuine emergencies involving their team, where reasonably required.

10. The minimum working hours specified for attendance are:
    - **Half day:** 4 hours and 30 minutes.
    - **Full day:** 7 hours.

    If an employee works less than 4 hours and 30 minutes for a half-day arrangement, the day may be treated as a full-day LWP under the original policy.

11. Employees are not permitted to avail leave during the notice period under the original policy. Any leave taken during this period will be treated as LWP and may extend the notice period accordingly.

12. If a resigning employee has exhausted their Earned Leave entitlement, applicable excess leave amounts may be deducted from their final salary settlement, subject to applicable rules.

13. Team members should coordinate their leave plans to ensure adequate team coverage. The original policy states that the total number of employees present should not fall below a ratio of 2:3 for any team at a given time.

14. Employees proceeding on leave must communicate their responsibilities and arrange a handover to another team member.

15. Employees who need to extend approved leave must submit a written request, provide the reason and supporting evidence where applicable, and obtain approval before the originally sanctioned leave expires.

16. A maximum of 10 days of leave may be clubbed together, including official holidays and weekly offs. Earned Leave will be deducted only for applicable working days.

17. All Saturdays are weekly offs except the last Saturday of the month, which is designated for training and development.
    - Employees must attend the last Saturday's training and development activities.
    - A minimum of seven working hours is required for that Saturday to count as a full working day.
    - Hours worked on the last Saturday will not be included in the monthly average working-hour calculation.

18. Leave applications must be submitted at least four days before the intended leave date.

19. The original policy states that employees who fail to serve the required notice period will forfeit their right to receive experience letters or similar certificates from the organization. The application of this clause is subject to applicable law and company procedures.

20. In the event of termination by the organization, the original policy specifies that the employee will receive a termination letter and no other employment letters.

## 5. After Resignation Policy

1. The standard notice period is **two months**, subject to the applicable employment terms.

2. Salary for the notice period will be processed in the subsequent payroll cycle, in accordance with the company's payroll procedures.

3. Employees who have been working from home and subsequently submit their resignation may be required to return to the office to serve their notice period, as stated in the original policy.

4. The relieving letter and experience letter will be provided one month after the employee leaves the organization, as specified in the original policy and subject to applicable procedures.

5. Employees requiring assistance with EPF-related activities, including withdrawal or other formalities, will be supported after 90 days from their date of separation, as stated in the original policy.

6. If an employee submits a resignation within four months of the increment cycle, the increment may not be considered. The salary may be calculated based on the pre-increment amount, and the original policy reserves the right for an authorized individual to deduct the incremented amount where considered necessary.

7. If an employee breaches a service agreement before completing the agreed duration, the original policy states that salary and employment documents may not be provided.

**Note:** The provisions concerning salary withholding, deductions, forfeiture of employment documents, and notice-period requirements should be reviewed by HR against the employee's employment agreement and applicable law before enforcement.

## 6. Reporting Policy

1. Employees must report their completed work to their Team Leader (TL) or Project Coordinator through verbal communication and email.

2. Employees must ensure that their work submissions are reviewed and address any questions, clarifications, or follow-up requirements raised by the relevant stakeholders.

3. Employees are responsible for keeping their reporting manager informed of work progress and any issues that may affect delivery.

## 7. Assets Policy

1. A charge of **₹100** will apply for the replacement of a lost punch card, as specified in the original policy.

2. Employees are responsible for maintaining their assigned seats and chairs in proper condition and ensuring that the area is left tidy after use.

3. Employees must help conserve electricity by:
   - Switching off washroom and pantry lights after use.
   - Shutting down their computer systems before leaving for the day.
   - Switching off the designated main power switch where applicable.

   Failure to follow these instructions may result in a penalty after three warnings, as stated in the original policy.

4. Employees are responsible for the electronic equipment assigned to them. Any damage may result in a penalty in accordance with company procedures.

5. Employees must maintain cleanliness at their desks. The original policy prohibits eating food items in the work premises.

6. Employees are responsible for safeguarding their personal valuables.

7. Bags, tiffin boxes, helmets, and other personal accessories must be stored properly in the designated canteen room.

## 8. Punch Card Policy

1. Each employee is responsible for their own punch card and must keep it safe.

2. When entering the office, employees must punch their card at the designated **IN** machine.

3. When leaving the office, employees must punch their card at the designated **OUT** machine.

4. Employees are permitted to forget or omit their punch card only once per month, as specified in the original policy.

5. A charge of ₹100 will apply for a replacement card if the card is lost.

6. If an employee forgets their card at the office, the card will be kept under HR's control. A charge of ₹100 is specified in the original policy for retrieving it.

7. Employees must carry their own punch card whenever required.

8. Employees must punch their cards carefully to avoid incorrect or missing attendance records.

### 8.1 Miss-Punch

1. Employees may submit a maximum of **two miss-punch requests per month** through NIMS.

2. Miss-punch requests must be submitted through:

   **NIMS → Time Track → Miss-Punch**

3. A request may be submitted on the following day or within a maximum of two days from the date of the missed punch.

4. Miss-punch requests may be approved on the same day or within a maximum of two days from the date of the missed punch.

### 8.2 Forgotten Punch Card

1. Employees may submit a maximum of **one forgotten-card request per month** through NIMS.

2. Requests must be submitted through:

   **NIMS → Time Track → Forgot Card**

3. Employees may submit the request on the following day only.

4. Forgotten-card requests may be approved on the same day the request is submitted.
